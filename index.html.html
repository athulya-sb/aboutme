After completing my PhD in Child Psychology from the University of Edinburgh, I specialized in treating behavioral disorders in children and emotional regulation in adults. I have had the privilege of working with the Kerala Government on several mental health initiatives and maintaining a thriving practice at the Community Wellness Center in Kochi.

The Consultation Process

A simple, structured approach to beginning your journey toward improved mental wellbeing.

Initial Contact

Reach out through the contact form, email, or phone to schedule your initial consultation. Share brief information about your concerns to help me prepare for our first meeting.

1
2

First Consultation

A 60-minute session to discuss your concerns in detail, explore your history, and identify goals for therapy. This session helps us determine the most appropriate approach for your needs.

Personalized Treatment Plan

Based on our initial consultation, I'll develop a customized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs, including recommended therapy approaches, frequency of sessions, and estimated timeline.

3
4

Ongoing Sessions

Regular therapy sessions (typically 45-60 minutes) to work toward your goals. Sessions can be conducted in-person at my office or online via secure video conferencing, based on your preference.

Progress Evaluation

Regular assessment of your progress toward therapy goals, with adjustments to the treatment plan as needed. Open communication ensures that therapy remains effective and aligned with your evolving needs.
